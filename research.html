Research

Four lines of work

All four share one method: solve the electromagnetic problem analytically, verify it in full-wave simulation, then build it and put it in a scanner.

01 / MRI

Metasurface add-ons for clinical MRI

Thin, flexible, wearable resonant wraps that locally boost the transmit and receive magnetic field at 63.87 MHz. Spiral quartets, coupled octa-spirals, interconnected rectangular windings — all designed to raise SNR on an unmodified 1.5 T scanner.

02 / RF

RF coils across field strengths

Transmit and receive coil design, tuning, matching, prototyping and phantom validation from portable 50 mT systems through clinical 1.5 T to ultrahigh-field 7 T and 9.4 T — including bow-tie antenna arrays for thermal MR at 297 MHz.

03 / Thermal MR

Target-conformal thermal magnetic resonance

Reconfigurable metamaterial applicators that steer RF energy toward a tumour while suppressing surface hotspots. Time-multiplexed beamforming and SAR-constrained optimisation for extremity sarcoma and brain tumours.

04 / Photonics

Nanophotonics & plasmonic sensing

Electro-tunable metasurfaces for polarisation switching, energy-saving all-weather windows, and fluoride-fibre surface-plasmon-resonance sensors using multilayer 2D materials — the optical end of the same physics.
